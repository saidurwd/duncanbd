# Website administration: editor's guide

Sign in at `https://duncanbd.com/<admin-path>` (your administrator gives you the address). You need your password and,
once two-factor authentication is on, the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. What you see in the menu depends on
your role.

## Roles in one line each
Super Admin (everything, at most two people) · Site Admin (settings, menus, publishing) · Editor (publishes pages and news) ·
Author (writes drafts) · IR / Company Secretary (investor documents) · HR (careers and applications) · Viewer (read only).

## Your account
- **My security** (user menu, top right): change your password (12+ characters, mixed case, a number) and manage two-factor authentication.
- Lost your phone? A Super Admin resets your two-factor from **Users & roles**; you set it up again at next sign-in.
- Forgot your password? Use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page.

## Pages (Pages)
1. **New page**, give it a title; choose a parent to place it in the menu structure.
2. Add **content blocks** with the "Add block" list: Rich text, Hero (full-width photo slider), Quick links, Key figures, Cards, Gallery, Document list, News feed, Timeline, FAQ, Call to action and more. Use the arrows to reorder.
3. In rich-text blocks use the toolbar: headings (H2–H4), bold, lists, links (https://…, /page-path, mailto:).
4. **Save** as often as you like; every save is a **revision** (Revisions → Restore).
5. **Preview** shows the draft to colleagues through a one-hour private link.
6. Publishing flow: Draft → **In review** → **Approved** → **Published** (or **Scheduled** for a future date). Authors can submit for review; Editors approve and publish. Nothing is public until published.
7. Changing a page's address (slug) keeps old links working for a published page.
Images in blocks use a **media ID**: find it in **Media library** (hover/edit the image).

## Media library
Upload JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF (up to 10 MB). **Alt text is required** unless you tick "decorative": describe what the picture shows, in one short sentence. Add a credit for photographers. Folders keep things tidy.

## News & press, Documents, Careers
- **News & press**: write a story, set category and image, then Publish (or schedule a date). The home page and News list update by themselves.
- **Documents** (reports, notices, policies): upload a PDF/Word/Excel/PowerPoint file (up to 50 MB), choose the category and financial year. Uploading again adds a **new version**; old versions are kept. "Investor" categories are for IR / Company Secretary only. Public files are served through the download link, never a direct file address.
- **Careers**: create a vacancy (closing date closes it automatically); applications arrive in **Applications** with the CV (download is logged). Move candidates through the pipeline and export a CSV.

## Company content
Businesses, Estates, Board & leadership and the History timeline are managed under **Company content**. They feed the Products pages, estate pages and timeline blocks.

## Menus, Settings, redirects
- **Menus**: the "Explore" menu (two levels), footer and legal links. Number the parent to nest an item.
- **Settings**: site name, contact details, social links, cookie banner text, analytics ID, SMTP mail, enquiry inboxes per category, default meta description.
- **Redirects & 404s**: add a redirect when you move or delete something; the list of 404s shows what visitors could not find.

## Enquiries and subscribers
Enquiries from the contact form land in **Enquiries**, are emailed to the inbox set for their category, and the sender gets an acknowledgement. Set status and assign them. **Subscribers** shows the confirmed mailing list (double opt-in); export is for confirmed addresses only.

## Privacy tools (Super Admin / Site Admin)
Look up everything held about an email address, download it as JSON, or erase it on request. Job applications are deleted automatically 12 months after a vacancy closes and enquiries after 24 months.

## Good practice
Write for readers first; keep headings in order (H2 then H3); never paste text copied from Word without checking the formatting; give every image alt text; check the **Preview** on a phone before publishing; ask a colleague to review anything price-sensitive or statutory.
